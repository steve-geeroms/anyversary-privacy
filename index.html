Privacy policy for any.vers.ary

Last updated: 2026-10-18

Short version: any.vers.ary does not collect, send or share any data about you. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

This policy explains what the app stores, what it does not do, and where data can still travel when you choose to send it somewhere yourself. The app is made by Steve Geeroms, an individual developer in Belgium.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your Seeds: the names, dates and (optionally) times you enter.
  • Your reminders: the milestones you ask the app to remind you of.
  • Your settings: which values the screens show.
  • Whether you own Plus: a simple yes or no, so the app does not ask the store every time.

All of this is kept only on your phone, in the app's own storage. The developer has no access to it and never receives it. There is no account, no sign-in and no server.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect analytics, usage statistics or crash reports.
  • It does not show advertisements and contains no advertising or tracking code.
  • It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any personal information.
  • It does not connect to the internet for its own purposes. Its font is part of the app, and nothing is downloaded while you use it. The only network use is the store check described under "Buying Plus".

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone by the operating system. The app asks for permission to show notifications the first time you add a reminder. Nothing about your reminders is sent anywhere. You can switch the permission off at any time in your phone's settings.

Buying Plus

Plus is a one-time purchase handled completely by Google Play or the Apple App Store, under their own privacy policies. The app never sees your payment details. It only learns from the store that the purchase was made, and remembers that on your phone.

When the app starts it quietly asks the store whether your account already owns Plus, so that a purchase follows you to a new phone. For this, the store's own software inside the app (Google Play Billing on Android, StoreKit on iPhone) talks to Google or Apple, and it may exchange technical data with them (for example the app version and diagnostics) under their privacy policies. The app sends them none of your Seeds, reminders or settings, and the developer receives nothing.

When you choose to share or save something

The app can create a picture card, a text or a backup file of your Seeds. These are created only when you tap the button, and you decide where they go through your phone's share sheet (a chat app, mail, Drive, Files, ...). From that moment the data is handled by the app you chose, under its policy. The developer never receives it.

Send feedback (in Settings) opens your own mail app with a draft addressed to the developer. At the end of the draft the app adds its version, the platform (Android or iOS) and the language of the app, so you can see what is included and delete it. Nothing is sent until you send the mail yourself; the app itself sends nothing.

A backup file contains your Seeds and reminders in readable text. Keep it somewhere you trust, as you would any personal document.

Device backups

Your phone's own backup (Google's backup on Android, iCloud backup on iPhone) may include the app's data if you have turned that on. That is controlled by you in your phone's settings and handled by Google or Apple.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request or erase on a server. You can delete any Seed inside the app, and uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone. For data in a backup file or in your phone's backup, delete that file or backup.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes what it does with data, this policy will be updated first and the change described in the release notes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
